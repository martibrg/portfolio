sepulchrum (in loving memory)

Death has always been a taboo; humans have spent their entire existence trying to accept it and to narrate it. This work needs human interaction to come to life; grief and memory become a sensorial experience born from the relationship between the spectator and the work.

An Arduino module stands on top of a chunk of soil that resembles both a computer tower and the negative of a hole in the ground, waiting for the right input to show some signs of life, LEDs flickering, printing out a short exclamation like “Hello world!”, just to fulfill its role as a coping machine.

interactive environmental installation
polystyrene box covered with soil
and PVA glue, Arduino circuit
blender, ElevenLabs
47x45x25 cm circa
