Each camera mediates reality in its own way; the webcam placed in a public space, transmits the scene as if it was not captured and genuine, with no interference of human decisions in the montage or in the framing, while the cams in our phones help us capturing anything we can fit in the 16:9 format that is conquering most of the communication medias, leaving always something outside of our attention in favour of something that feels more deserving of attention.

Nothing is free from mediation and nobody is free from influence.
