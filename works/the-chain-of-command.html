the chain of command

This work portrays the chronically online world, with its countless inside jokes and colorful memes that burn and “rot” the brains of those immersed in it. Living constantly online means navigating this chaos without fear: every symbol carries a shared meaning and poses no threat, unlike what can happen in the experience of the physical world.

This digital garden, filled with gifs and trapped in an endless loop, is an artificial paradise where the fear of damnation as punishment for excesses isn’t made explicit, but instead hidden beneath a veil of irony, typical of those who live online.
