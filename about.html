Martina Borgese was born in Fano (Italy) in 2001, and lives and works in Milan. Her projects address technological ambiguity, alternative visions, and digital cultures through a practice built on experiments with different software and media, seeking to develop works that can exist in various final forms, both digital and non-digital. Attached to the connections she has built through research and artistic practice, she seeks to create new ones through her own work. She is enrolled in the Second Level Specialisation Course in Multimedia Arts - Net Art at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera. She has taken part in several group exhibitions, including Come frammenti nell'assurdo (Fondazione Ago, Modena 2026) and An Ecology of Beasts (TXT Studio & BASE Milano, 2026).
