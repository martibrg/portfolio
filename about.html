Martina Borgese was born in Fano (Italy) in 2001, and lives and works in Milan. During her studies she met many personalities from the art world who guided her research and her practice, inspiring her inner geek side to try new materials and languages without fear. Every day she chases the white rabbit on a journey of curiosity, feeding her head and developing new works that address issues such as the ambiguities of technological development and phenomena linked to digital cultures.
